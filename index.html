How this site handles inquiries

Is a load still damp after a complete drying cycle?

If a Cambridge dryer vent runs from a basement through a long rise, several elbows, or a finished wall, lint can collect along sections that are not visible from the appliance. A useful inspection follows the route from the dryer connection to the exterior hood and checks the termination as well as the pipe. Before arranging service, note the dryer location, photograph visible vent sections, and ask how the full route and airflow will be assessed.
